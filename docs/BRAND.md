# SAANVIKA TECHNOLOGIES — Brand Guide

To view every logo variation and the palette in one place, open `docs/brand-sheet.html` through the local preview server.

## The mark

A geometric **S monogram** built from two interlocking circuit traces:

- **Upper trace (Midnight Navy):** starts at a circular *via* node, runs left, steps down at 45° and returns to the centre.
- **Lower trace (Premium Teal):** mirrors the upper trace through 180°, ending in its own via node.

The two centre bars run in parallel like a data bus. Together they stand for the two sides of the business: *repair* and *support*. The mark sits on a 64-unit grid with 6-unit strokes and rounded joins, so it stays legible down to 16 px.

The lockups use live SVG text set in Inter, falling back to Segoe UI or Arial. For print or signage, convert the text to outlines in a vector editor (Illustrator, Figma, Inkscape) with Inter installed.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space: keep at least the height of one via node × 2 empty around the logo on every side.
- Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen for the monogram, 120 px wide for the full lockup.
- Don't recolour the traces, rotate the mark, add effects or shadows, or stretch it.

## Colour palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Navy | `#101D35` | Primary brand colour, headings, dark sections |
| Premium Teal | `#18B7A7` | Accent only: buttons, icons, highlights |
| Graphite | `#243247` | Body text |
| Soft White | `#F5F7FA` | Section backgrounds |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Base background |
| Muted Gray | `#778397` | Secondary labels |

**Accessibility:** teal on white does not meet contrast requirements for small text. For small teal text on light backgrounds, use the darker `#0B7A70`. On teal buttons, use navy text.

## Typography

**Inter**, weights 400, 500, 600 and 700.
- Headings: 700, tight tracking (−0.025em to −0.04em)
- Body: 400, line-height 1.65
- Labels/eyebrows: 600, uppercase, +0.12em tracking

## Voice

Clear, calm and practical. Explain, don't oversell. Avoid superlatives and any claims the business can't back up.
