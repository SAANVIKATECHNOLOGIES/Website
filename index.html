    AEO / Quick Answers

    Frequently Asked Questions.

    Clear, direct information about our hardware sales, repairs, on-site services, and AMC maintenance contracts.

    Do you provide on-site technical service across Visakhapatnam?
    Yes. Our certified field engineers provide on-site inspection, computer repair, CCTV setup, network cabling, and biometric installation across Madhurawada, Rushikonda IT SEZ, Brandix, MVP Colony, Gajuwaka, and all surrounding Vizag areas.
    What equipment is covered under an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)?
    We offer both Comprehensive (including replacement parts) and Non-Comprehensive AMCs. Contracts can cover Desktop PCs, Laptops, Printers, CCTV surveillance systems, Biometric attendance terminals, Office Wi-Fi networks, and Intercom PBX systems with routine monthly checkups.
    Do you sell genuine brand-new computers and IT security equipment?
    Yes. We supply genuine branded desktops (Dell, HP, Lenovo), high-performance laptops, commercial HD/IP surveillance cameras (Hikvision, CP Plus, Dahua), biometric attendance devices, and commercial routers with manufacturer warranty and full initial setup.
    Can you set up mobile remote viewing for existing CCTV cameras?
    Yes. We configure port forwarding, cloud P2P, and mobile apps (iOS & Android) so you can view live and recorded security footage from anywhere in the world on your smartphone or laptop.
    Do you execute structured network cabling for new offices and server racks?
    Yes. We design and install complete enterprise LAN cabling (CAT6 and Fiber), patch panel terminations, cable management, 4U to 42U server rack dressing, and commercial Wi-Fi 6 access point installations.
    Do you undertake projects outside Visakhapatnam in Andhra Pradesh?
    Yes. We execute multi-location commercial deployments, CCTV projects, and IT AMCs for clients in Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Paderu, and Araku.
