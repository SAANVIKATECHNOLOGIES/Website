Frequently Asked Questions About IT AMCs.

How is the annual price for an IT AMC calculated?
Pricing is calculated based on the total inventory count (number of Desktops, Laptops, Printers, Cameras, Switches), the age of the equipment, and whether you choose a Comprehensive (with parts) or Non-Comprehensive contract. We conduct a free preliminary site audit to provide a transparent quotation.
Do you offer customized AMCs for residential apartment societies?
Yes. We provide specialized residential society AMCs focused on CCTV surveillance cameras, security gate intercom systems, clubhouse biometric access, and entry boom barriers.
Do you provide AMC support across Andhra Pradesh cities?
Yes. We support corporate organizations with branch networks across Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Paderu, and Araku under unified service level agreements.
