Frequently Asked Questions About CCTV Systems.

How many days of video recording can a CCTV hard drive store?
Recording duration depends on the number of cameras, resolution (2MP, 4MP, 4K), and hard drive capacity. Generally, a 1TB surveillance drive stores 15-20 days for 4 cameras, while 2TB-4TB drives provide 30 to 60+ days of continuous rolling footage using modern H.265+ compression.
Do your CCTV cameras record clear video at night in complete darkness?
Yes. We install advanced Infrared (IR) night vision and full-color night cameras (ColorVu / Full-Color technology) that provide crystal-clear daytime color imaging even in zero-lux pitch darkness.
Do you execute CCTV projects in Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, and Kakinada?
Yes. In addition to 20+ areas in Greater Visakhapatnam, our project teams execute commercial surveillance installations across Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Paderu, and Araku.
