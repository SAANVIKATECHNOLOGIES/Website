AEO / Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Biometric Systems.

What happens to the magnetic EM lock during a power cut or fire emergency?
Safety regulations require "fail-safe" EM locks to release when power is cut, ensuring safe emergency evacuation. For routine power continuity, we install 12V battery backup power supplies that keep the door locked for 4-8 hours during normal load shedding.
Can employees check their attendance on their mobile phones?
Yes. With our cloud-connected biometric solutions, employees and managers can view real-time check-in/out timestamps and apply for leaves via secure Android and iOS mobile apps.
Do you serve clients in Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, and Kakinada?
Yes. We install and maintain biometric access control systems for factories, offices, and colleges across Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Paderu, and Araku.
