Frequently Asked Questions About Firewalls & Power.

Can a hardware firewall block employees from browsing social media or gaming sites during office hours?
Yes. Advanced firewalls feature Application Control and Web Filtering. You can selectively block or schedule access to social media, video streaming, gambling, and peer-to-peer downloading sites while giving full speed to productivity tools.
How often should Online UPS batteries be replaced?
High-quality SMF sealed lead-acid batteries typically last 3 to 4 years under normal operating temperatures. We provide regular quarterly battery impedance testing to identify and replace individual dying battery cells before the entire power bank fails.
