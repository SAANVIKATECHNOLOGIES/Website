Frequently Asked Questions About Printers & Toners.

Is it better to buy an InkTank printer or a LaserJet printer?
If you print documents daily and require color printing at an ultra-low running cost (approx. 10-20 paise per page), an InkTank printer is ideal. If you primarily print sharp black-and-white text documents at high speed with zero risk of ink drying out during weeks of non-use, a monochrome LaserJet printer is the superior choice.
Does refilling a laser toner cartridge damage the printer?
No, provided it is done professionally using the exact micro-fine toner powder suited to your printer model, along with cleaning the waste toner hopper and inspecting the OPC drum. We ensure clean, mess-free toner cartridges that deliver pristine prints.
