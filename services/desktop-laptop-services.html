How much does a laptop or desktop diagnosis cost?

How can I make my slow computer or laptop faster?
The most effective upgrade is replacing an older mechanical hard drive with a high-speed Solid State Drive (SSD) and upgrading RAM. This delivers up to 5x-10x faster boot times and responsive multi-tasking without buying a new system.
