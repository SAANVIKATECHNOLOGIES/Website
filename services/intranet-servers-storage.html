Frequently Asked Questions About Servers & Data Recovery.

Can our remote employees access the office NAS server from home?
Yes. We configure secure encrypted VPNs (Virtual Private Networks) or SSL-secured private cloud access so authenticated remote employees can securely access files from home without exposing your server to public threats.
What should I do if my hard drive is making clicking noises or not turning on?
Immediately unplug the power and stop using the drive. Repeatedly powering on a clicking or mechanically damaged hard drive causes permanent platter scratching and unrecoverable data loss. Contact SAANVIKA TECHNOLOGIES for a safe clean diagnostic evaluation.
