Frequently Asked Questions About Intercom Systems.

Can an apartment intercom prevent neighbors from listening in on calls?
Yes. Our modern intercom systems feature full speech privacy and caller secrecy. Once a flat answers a call from the security gate or another flat, the line is digitally locked and cannot be overheard or interrupted by other extensions.
What is a Video Door Phone (VDP) and how does it work?
A Video Door Phone combines an outdoor camera-integrated bell at your door and a color LCD display inside your home. When a visitor rings, you can see their live video, speak with them two-way, and unlock the electronic door lock from the screen.
